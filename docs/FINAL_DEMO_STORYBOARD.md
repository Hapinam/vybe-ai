## Story

1. **One fictional match, three experiences:** introduce the inclusive fan concept.
2. **Young Fan:** celebrations, accessible football explanations and interactive elements.
3. **Accessible Fan:** captions, controls, language options and spoken commentary.
4. **Analyst Fan:** synthetic event statistics, timeline and momentum.
5. **Microsoft Foundry:** evidence-grounded Ask the Match responses and model-assisted narration without inventing match facts.

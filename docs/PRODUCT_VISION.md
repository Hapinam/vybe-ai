# VYBE — Product Vision

**Tagline:** Every Fan Belongs.

## Mission
Make football understandable, engaging, and accessible to fans of different ages, abilities, languages, and levels of expertise.

## Problem
Conventional match dashboards optimize for statistics, not people. A young fan, an analyst, and a fan relying on accessible commentary need different explanations of the same event.

## Core promise
One synthetic match event produces consistent, grounded narratives tailored to different fan needs.

## Initial personas
1. **Young Fan:** friendly explanations without jargon or patronizing language.
2. **Accessible Commentary:** clear, structured, screen-reader-friendly updates with optional text-to-speech.
3. **Analyst:** recorded-event insights with evidence and uncertainty; no unsupported tactical interpretation.

## Trust and inclusion principles
- All match events are synthetic; never imply real live Premier League data.
- The statistics engine is deterministic; AI must not invent goals, passes, players, or probabilities.
- Every narrative links back to supporting event IDs and computed metrics.
- Accessible commentary is an assistive feature, not a substitute for a complete professional audio-description service.
- Support keyboard navigation, sufficient contrast, screen readers, and user-controlled pace.
- Personalization adapts style, not facts; avoid inferring emotions or disabilities.

## Demo story
At minute 87 of a synthetic match, Team A equalizes 2–2. Switch between Young Fan, Accessible Commentary, and Analyst profiles while the underlying score and facts remain unchanged.

## Success criteria
- Synthetic event playback reliably updates score and match timeline.
- Each supported profile explains the same event without factual contradiction.
- Explanations include evidence references.
- A keyboard-only user can navigate the demo.
- A public, reproducible demo and a short English video are ready for judging.
